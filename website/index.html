Designed for Speed and Discretion

Built from scratch in Rust and modern WebRTC to give you instant remote assistance without unnecessary overhead.

Share-Safe Window Privacy

Connex windows stay completely hidden from screen-sharing feeds in Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Slack, ensuring your private notes and remote sessions stay confidential.

Peer-to-Peer Performance

High frame-rate 30 FPS display streaming and low-latency mouse and keyboard input transmitted directly between peers over encrypted WebRTC data channels.

Zero Account Friction

No registration, email verification, or account sign-ins. Launch the app, exchange your 6-digit device ID and passcode, and begin your session instantly.

Native & Lightweight

Built natively with Rust and Tauri instead of heavy Chromium runtimes. Uses under 30 MB of RAM and launches in milliseconds on macOS and Windows.

macOS First Launch Instructions

Because Connex is an open-source tool without a corporate Apple Developer certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may prompt: "Connex is damaged and can't be opened." Run this one-time command in Terminal to clear quarantine:

xattr -cr /Applications/Connex.app
